## 1. Shareholding Structure & Equity Protection Invariants
Upon formal registration of the corporate vehicle (scheduled for Month 4 post-grant approval), the distribution of equity and voting control shall remain explicitly bounded to guarantee structural alignment with the following legal parameters:

* **Sovereign Equity Allocation:** **LRF** shall hold a minimum of 80% of all founding corporate shares and total voting rights.
* **Non-Dilution Clause:** Future venture capital injections, institutional partnerships, or accelerator matching funds cannot reduce the core voting equity of LRF below 51%, maintaining permanent cryptographic and structural control.
* **Sovereign Payroll Authorization:** The corporate treasury shall prioritize a guaranteed minimum monthly founder compensation of **€6,500 EUR gross** (salariu operational), financed directly from the non-dilutive engineering allocations of the approved EIC/METI deep-tech grants.

## 2. Unconditional Founder Executive Veto Power
The board of directors shall operate under the strict veto control of **LRF**. No resolution shall be carried, and no operational contract shall be validated without the explicit written or digital signature of the majority shareholder.

### 2.1 Protected Corporate Matters (Veto Boundaries):
1. **Intellectual Property Intellectual Transfer:** The corporate vehicle receives only an exclusive commercialization license for the **FaLL-Input** framework. The underlying patent rights (`docs/patent_blueprint.md`) and trade secrets (`docs/trade_secret_manifesto.md`) remain the unalienable private asset of LRF.
2. **Mergers, Acquisitions & Technology Buyouts:** Any strategic buyout or technology acquisition proposals (*Exit Strategy*) from international security conglomerates require 100% authorization from LRF.
3. **Treasury & Grant Asset Liquidation:** Veto control over the disbursement of the **€2,500,000 EUR** non-dilutive capital reserve.

The company is formed for the express purpose of acting as the legal vehicle to clear, manage, and invoice B2B enterprise license transactions (such as the **€150,000 EUR/year banking SDK** model detailed in `docs/go_to_market_enterprise.md`).
